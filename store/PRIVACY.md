# Video Popup Guard: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Video Popup Guard is a browser extension that stops pop-up ads, invisible click-stealing layers and unwanted redirects on video websites, and can redirect video players hosted on offline domains to working ones.

## Summary

**Video Popup Guard does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. Nothing you do is ever sent off your device.**

## What the extension accesses, and why

To do its job, the extension runs on the web pages you visit while protection is switched on:

- **Page content and events.** It watches for pop-up attempts, invisible layers covering a video, and ad scripts that intercept your clicks or key presses, and neutralizes them. It also lets your keyboard shortcuts (play/pause, seek, volume, full screen, mute) control the video on the page.
- **Tab and navigation information.** It notices when a page opens a new tab or window, or redirects the video tab to another website, so it can close the unwanted tab or send you back to the video.
- **Web requests for video players.** If you turn on "Fix dead player domains", it redirects requests for domains on your list to the working domain you chose.

All of this happens locally in your browser.

## What is stored

Only your settings, kept in your browser's local extension storage:

- whether protection is on or off,
- your list of domain redirects and whether each one is on,
- temporary per-tab counters of blocked items (shown on the toolbar icon; cleared when the tab is closed or moves to a new page).

This data never leaves your device, and removing the extension deletes it.

## What is not collected

- No browsing history, page contents, form input, or keystrokes are recorded or transmitted.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party services are used.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code.
